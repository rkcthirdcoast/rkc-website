Why RKC

The pathway

College players spend their summer here competing for a shot at the next level.

The community

Clubs at this level don't run on big budgets. They run on volunteers, interns, local businesses and fans who keep showing up.

The lake

Lake Michigan defines this region, so looking after it is part of the job. We run monthly cleanups at Eichelman Park in Kenosha.
